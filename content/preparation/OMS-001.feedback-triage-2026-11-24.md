## 4. Corrections, caution and residual uncertainties

- The supplied UK SDCEP guidance is a credible technical reference, but applicability to Indian practice, regulatory consent, escalation services and local drug labels remains to be reviewed by a dentist competent in the context.
- INR <4 and 24/72-hour timings are not independent patient-care instructions. A sample anticoagulation scenario must identify whether the medicine is a VKA, DOAC, antiplatelet or combination. For complex/high-risk cases, coordinate with appropriate treating clinician; never teach automatic discontinuation.
- The submitted description of primary/reactionary/secondary bleeding may have temporal terminology differences across textbooks. Prefer the SDCEP's verified *immediate / within hours / within a week* presentation language; precise nosological labels require edition-specific review.
- Clinical reference links verify that a source discusses the concept. They do not approve the **exact** current lesson/MCQ/case explanation, distractor correctness, safety, diagram labeling or Indian clinical use.
- No blanket “all MCQ answers correct” conclusion is supported by the submission. The original marked option in each MCQ remains only a legacy draft key. New candidate keys require item-by-item human review.
