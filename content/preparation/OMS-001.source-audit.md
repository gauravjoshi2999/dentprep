## 4. Item-specific problems found

### Existing lesson `cd7a294f...`

- Existing `notes_md` consists of a short generic safety paragraph and a single anticoagulant vignette; there is no detailed sequenced, illustrated, objective-by-objective 5–15-minute teaching module.
- Objectives are generic (`Identify the main clinical concepts`, `Explain safe assessment and relevant referral decisions`), not observable/assessable prerequisites or a complete exodontia syllabus.
- `references` is empty; the only viva prompt asks which information is needed before deciding how to proceed, without a sourced answer guide or feedback rubric.
- No graded hint, reviewed explanation/remediation, distinct fresh practice, transfer case or delayed retention items are linked.
- **Required:** reviewer-defined learning outcomes, claim-level locators, scoped decisions, source permissions, educational corrections and assessment mapping before any publication.

### Existing question `6fac07ab...`: pre-extraction checks

- Current marked answer is the option describing correct patient, tooth, indication and consent. That is a useful partial checklist, but the wording *what must be verified* suggests an exhaustive answer despite omitting medical risk assessment, allergies, medicines and clinical suitability.
- The other three options are implausibly weak. This may measure obvious test-taking rather than extraction-assessment understanding.
- **Revision candidate for a qualified reviewer:** specify a bounded scenario, include medically relevant assessment in the best answer, create clinically plausible distractors, and source the rationale for each option. Keep present stem/options blocked until accepted.

### Existing question `dc25b46f...`: ongoing post-extraction bleeding

- Current marked answer favors assessment, local haemostasis and escalation if needed. The draft does not distinguish brisk uncontrolled bleeding, systemic compromise, delayed bleeding or anticoagulant use.
- The very weak distractors (*ignore*, *another extraction*, automatic antibiotics) provide limited discrimination.
- **Revision candidate for a qualified reviewer:** explicit setting/severity, separate prompt vs emergency referral, a clinically specific but safely bounded best-answer rule, documented uncertainty/contraindications, reviewed rationale and fresh test variants.
- **Never** use this current question as a patient-facing emergency guide.

### Existing case `9ca96a5b...`: extraction request while on anticoagulant

- Vignette only says the patient is taking anticoagulant medication. Critical details absent: identity of agent(s), reason for therapy, current medication schedule, concurrent medications, relevant medical conditions, bleeding history, proposed dental procedure/risk, clinical symptoms and care setting.
- Existing steps ask general history/priority/communication questions but do not differentiate what is unknown from what has been established. Without more details it cannot support one safe management choice.
- **Required:** reviewer-authored or reviewed branching scenario, explicit information-gathering prompts, drug/procedure-specific uncertainty, safe referral/red flags, assessment rubric, patient-friendly explanation and cross-check against India-applicable care pathways.
- Do **not** insert dose, timing or drug interruption instructions from an AI inference.
